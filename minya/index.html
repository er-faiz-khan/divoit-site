Questions owners ask

Will anything post without me?

Never. Every reply waits for your POST or EDIT. Reviews about refunds, safety, staff or anything legal are held and flagged for you.

What access do you need?

Manager access to your Google Business Profile so replies can be posted for you. We text you the steps, or call and we'll do it together. You can remove it anytime.

How does it sound like me?

We study your past replies on Google. When you edit a draft, we ask whether to make that change a habit, so drafts need fewer edits over time.

Do I need an app or a login?

No. Everything happens in your regular text messages, on any phone.

How do I pay or cancel?

Payment is through Stripe. It's month to month: text END PLAN and we'll stop at the end of your month.

Who is behind this?

Divoit AI was built by a business owner, for other business owners. Minya answered real restaurant reviews before it was offered to anyone else.
