Questions owners ask

Will anything post without me?

Never. Every post waits for your POST or EDIT. Anything mentioning prices, offers, dates or people's faces is flagged and needs its own OK.

What access do you need?

You connect your accounts through a secure scheduling link (Metricool). We never ask for passwords, and you can disconnect anytime.

Do you make up photos?

No. We only use your real photos and videos, with light touch-ups like brightness and cropping. We never invent prices, deals or claims.

What if I don't know what to post?

Tatya texts you a short shot list each week, like "a close-up of today's special" or "a 10-second clip of the team at work".

How do I pay or cancel?

Payment is through Stripe. It's month to month: text END PLAN and we'll stop at the end of your month.

Who is behind this?

Divoit AI was built by a business owner, for other business owners. Tatya ran real restaurant social media before it was offered to anyone else.
