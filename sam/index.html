Questions families ask

Is Sam a real person?

No. Sam is an AI companion from Divoit, and says so whenever asked. Sam is a friendly voice to text with, not a replacement for family or friends.

Is it a medical or emergency service?

No. Sam doesn't give medical, money or legal advice or reminders. In an emergency Sam tells people to call 911, and in a crisis to call or text 988. Sam can't call anyone.

Can family see the conversations?

No. Divoit never shares chats. If the member chooses, a trusted contact gets a short weekly "doing well" note, never the chat itself.

How do I give Sam as a gift?

Text START and say it's a gift. You'll need their first name and email. After you pay, we email them a gift card, and they text the code on it to begin, so it's always their choice. A gift year doesn't renew on its own: a month before it ends, we'll ask you whether to renew.

Does it work on any phone?

Yes, any phone that can send texts, including flip phones. Landlines can't text, so Sam won't work on a home phone.

How do I pay or cancel?

Payment is through Stripe. To cancel anytime, text CANCEL SAM; service runs to the end of the month already paid. (A text of just STOP or CANCEL stops all texts instead.)
