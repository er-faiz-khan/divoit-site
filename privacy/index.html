Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Divoit LLC ("Divoit", "we", "us"), based in Saint Paul, Minnesota, collects and uses information when you use our services, including Minya by Divoit (Google review replies) and Tatya by Divoit (social media posts), and our text messaging program.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: your name, business name, business address or service area, email address and mobile phone number.
  • Business details: your website, Google Business Profile and social media handles, and the public posts and reviews on them.
  • Messages, photos and videos you text us, and your replies to our drafts and previews.
  • Payment status from Stripe. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.

How we use it

  • To provide the service you signed up for: drafting review replies, making social posts, texting you drafts and previews, and posting or scheduling only what you approve.
  • To onboard you, send your payment link, confirm setup, and answer your questions.
  • To learn your preferred style from your edits so drafts need fewer changes over time.

Text messages (SMS)

You opt in to texts from Divoit by texting START to our number for the service you use. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent collected for our text program are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service providers

We use trusted providers only to run the service: Twilio (text delivery), Stripe (payments), Metricool (scheduling posts you approve), Google (Business Profile and email) and Dropbox (storing the media you send us). They may process your information only to provide their service to us.

Retention and your choices

We keep your information while you use our services and delete it on request after your plan ends, except where we must keep records by law. To access, correct or delete your information, email support@divoit.com.
