How it works

Calls ring you first

Your business line forwards to a local Nelya number. It rings your phone like normal, so you answer when you can.

II

Missed? Nelya texts back

If you can't pick up, the caller gets a friendly text from your business within seconds, and their voicemail is turned into text.

You get the lead

Nelya texts you who called and what they need. Reply from your phone and it goes to the customer. Leads that go quiet get a gentle reminder.

Questions owners ask

Do I have to change my number?

No. Your customers keep calling the number they know. Calls forward to your Nelya number, which rings you first.

What does the caller see?

A short, friendly text in your business's name. You choose the wording during setup.

Will it text people at night?

You decide. Nelya can text callers right away, and it holds your own lead alerts during your quiet hours unless something is urgent.

What about spam calls?

Reply SPAM to any lead and that number is blocked from future texts.

How do I pay or cancel?

Payment is through Stripe. It's month to month, with no refunds for partial months: cancel anytime and service runs to the end of your billing month.

Privacy and text messages

When someone calls a business that uses Nelya and the call is missed, Nelya may send that caller texts about their call on the business's behalf. Callers can reply STOP at any time to stop texts, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. Business owners receive lead alerts and can reply by text. Call details, voicemails and messages are used only to deliver the service to that business, are never sold, and are deleted 90 days after a lead closes. Questions: support@divoit.com.
